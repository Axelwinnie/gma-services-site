Custom business workflow systems

Who it's for

Businesses that need more than one workflow, still inside the tools they already use.

The problem

Two or three processes share the same information, and a single map is not the whole job.

What we set up

Connected Workflows covers up to 3 workflows and up to 4 connected apps, with a written scope and a fixed price before you pay. Larger systems are a custom scope, still not custom software. We do not build apps.

Deliverables

A shared log, failure alerts to a named person, a training session, runbooks, and walkthroughs. One revision round per workflow and a 30-day fix window.

Timeline

15 business days for Connected Workflows, from $1,200, after inputs are in. A larger scope gets its own written timeline before you pay.

Which tier fits

Connected Workflows, from $1,200. A bigger system is quoted in writing before you pay.
