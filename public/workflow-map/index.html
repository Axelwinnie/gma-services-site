What a Map is

A Workflow Map is a one-page write-up of one process in your shop. I list every step, who does it, what tool they use, and where it stalls. Then I tell you which steps I'd automate, which ones stay with a person, and a fixed price to build the first one.

You keep the page even if you never hire me to build anything.

What's included

  • One process mapped end to end
  • What stays human (you approve anything that goes to a customer)
  • Ranked list of what I'd automate first
  • Tool options built around apps you already use
  • Fixed price for a One Workflow Build off that Map
  • 1 revision round
  • Turnaround: 3 business days after I confirm fit

What do you need from me?

What comes in, what should come out, and who touches it today. One named person who can approve outputs, and 3 to 5 examples (redacted or made-up is fine).

What happens after the Map?

You can hire me to build it at the quoted fixed price, or build it yourself from the plan. You keep the Map either way.
