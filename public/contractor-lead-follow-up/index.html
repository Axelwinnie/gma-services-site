FAQ

Will it text my customers without me knowing?

No. You approve the wording first. A person checks anything that goes to a customer before it goes out.

Do I need a new CRM?

Not usually. The Workflow Map says whether your current tools can carry the follow-up, or whether a change is worth it.

What does it cost monthly?

There is no monthly fee from Granite Models Automations. You only keep paying for the tools you already use.
