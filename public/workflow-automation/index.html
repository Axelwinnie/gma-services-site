Who it's for

  • Owner-run service and trade businesses, contractors, and small offices (roughly 1-25 people)
  • Teams where one person re-types the same information into quotes, forms, or emails every week
  • Businesses already using Google Workspace, Microsoft 365, or similar tools

Probably not a fit if

  • the process involves health records, card numbers, or bank/payroll logins
  • you want a fully autonomous system that acts without anyone checking
  • you need enterprise IT procurement

Problems we help with

  • Quotes and proposals rebuilt from scratch from notes, emails, or line items
  • New inquiries that sit in an inbox until someone has time to sort them
  • Documents (bid packs, specs, forms) that someone has to read and summarize by hand
  • Follow-ups that slip because nobody owns them

We won't promise how many hours you'll save. The Workflow Map records how long the process takes you today, so you can measure it yourself afterwards.

FAQ

What does "AI workflow" mean here?

A set of steps that runs in your software when something happens: a form comes in, an email arrives, a file is dropped. Some steps use an AI model to draft or summarize. A person checks the result before it reaches a customer.

Which tools do you use?

Yours, first. The workflow is built in the tools you already use. We have not published a platform list. We never ask for passwords.

Will this replace my staff?

No. It takes repetitive steps off someone's plate, and a person still approves anything customer-facing.

How much time will it save?

We don't promise a number. The Workflow Map records how long the process takes you today, so you can compare afterwards.

Who owns the workflow?

You do. It's built in your accounts, and the runbook explains how to change it or turn it off.

What will it cost me each month?

Only your own tool subscriptions, paid directly to the vendors. The plan lists each vendor's published price. We don't charge a monthly fee.

What happens to my files?

They're used only for your job and deleted 30 days after handoff, or sooner if you ask in writing. We never ask for passwords, and we ask you to remove our access at handoff. Parts of the material you send may be processed by the AI tools we use, so we work from redacted or sample data wherever the job allows.

What if it breaks?

If the workflow doesn't do what the signed scope says, we fix it free within 14 days of handoff for a One Workflow Build, or 30 days for Connected Workflows. After that, fixes are quoted per request.

Can I get a refund?

Yes, in full if you cancel before kickoff. After kickoff, you get a full refund if we can't deliver what the signed scope describes, even after the included revision round or fix window. Changes you request to the scope, and delays of more than 30 days on your side, aren't refundable.
