FAQ

Do I need to pay for Zapier or Make myself?

Yes. You keep your own Zapier or Make subscription, paid to the vendor. I set the workflow up in your accounts. I do not sell those subscriptions.

Do you need my passwords?

No. Access is through your tool's own invite or sharing feature. I never ask for passwords.

Who owns the workflow?

You do. It lives in your accounts. At handoff I ask you to remove my access.
