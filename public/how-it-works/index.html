Discover

You describe one process. We reply by email with whether it fits, which tier, and a fixed price. Nothing is charged at this step.

Design

A Workflow Map is the one-page plan: steps, tools, what to automate, and what stays human. For a build, you approve a short written scope before work starts.

Implement

We set the workflow up in your accounts and test it on sample data. Check-ins are at kickoff and handoff. We never ask for passwords.

Train and launch

You get a runbook. Connected Workflows also includes a 30-minute training session. We ask you to remove our access.

Grow

You own the workflow. Another process can be a new map later. This offer does not include an ongoing retainer.

Rules that sit beside the steps

What you provide, how payment works, and what happens if inputs are late are written on the policies page. Refunds, revisions, and file deletion each have their own page.
