File Organizer

Every shop has the folder full of Scan_0007.pdf and IMG_4471.HEIC. Load the sample pile or drop in your own files. It suggests a clear name for each one, sorts them by job and type, and sets aside the copies and the ones it can't place so a person can decide.

Demo on fictional data. The sample files, jobs and vendors are made up. If you drop in your own files, only the names, sizes and dates are read, right here in your browser. Nothing gets opened, uploaded or saved, and nothing on your computer gets renamed.

1. Get a pile of files

Or drag files here. Only names and dates are read.

2. Rules

Naming convention: date_job_type_vendor, for example 2026-09-18_J2041-PineHollow_Invoice_NorthfieldSteel.pdf. Photos and drawings skip the vendor when the name doesn't have one. Folders go Job, then Type.

Each line is Name = words to look for, comma separated. Words starting with a dot match the file extension. Change anything, then hit Apply.
