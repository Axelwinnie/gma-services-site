Tool Ticket System

In the real thing, one program runs on a PC in the shop and everyone else opens it in a browser. Somebody reports a problem, it gets assigned and worked, and it's closed out with notes and photos. The tool crib side shows who has what, what's late coming back, and what's tagged out for repair. QA/QC checklists, job hazard analyses, requisitions, rental equipment and lockout/tagout all live in the same place and talk to each other.

Demo on fictional data. Ridgeline Steel Works, its people, tools and jobs are made up. Everything here lives in this browser tab only. Nothing is saved or sent, and it all resets when you reload. No login needed.

QA/QC inspections

Each part gets a checklist: fit-up, weld visual, dimensions, coating. Anything that fails or gets held can be written up as an NCR, which opens a rework ticket. Sign-off only works once every check passes.

Job hazard analysis

Break the task into steps, list the hazards and controls for each, pick the PPE, and have the crew sign on. Tickets marked as needing a JHA can't move to In progress until one is linked and signed.

Requisitions

Ask for material or parts for a job. The office approves it and it gets a PO number, or rejects it with a reason. Marking it received drops a note on the linked ticket.

Rental equipment

What's on rent, from who, for which job, and when it's due back. Costs run up daily at the sample rates shown. Anything due within a day gets a call off-rent alert so the shop doesn't pay for a day it didn't use.

Lockout/tagout

One record per machine with its energy sources. Apply locks, verify zero energy, release. Each person takes off only their own lock. While a lockout is on, the tool can't be checked out and its ticket can't be closed.

Analytics

Shop-wide view across every module, worked out live from the sample data in this tab. Filter by department to see just one crew.
