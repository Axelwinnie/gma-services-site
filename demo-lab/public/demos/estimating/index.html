Steel Estimating

Paste a member list and this figures the weight of every piece, the total tons, and a rough price at whatever rates you punch in. It's the first pass of the estimate packet I build for shops.

Demo. Sample pricing only. A person reviews every real packet before it goes out. Everything here runs in your browser, nothing gets saved or sent anywhere.

1. Member list

One piece per line. Mark, shape, qty, length. Commas, tabs, or spaces all work. Shapes like W8x31, HSS6x6x1/4, L4x4x3/8, C10x15.3, MC12x10.6, Pipe3STD, PL1/2x6. Length as 20'-6", 20'-6 1/2", 20' or 20.5.

Weights come from a built-in table of common AISC shapes. W, C, MC and WT use the weight in the name. Plate is figured at 490 lb per cubic foot. If a shape isn't in the table it gets flagged and left blank, not guessed. No connections, bolts, paint or freight in here. The real packet covers those.
