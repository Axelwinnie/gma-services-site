Try the tools. Built for the trades.

  • Live

    Tool Ticket System

    One program on one shop PC. Everyone else opens it in a browser. Maintenance tickets go from request to closed job, with notes, photos, and a clear status at every step. The tool crib, QA/QC inspections and NCRs, job hazard analyses, requisitions, rental equipment and lockout/tagout live in the same place, with a shop-wide analytics page on top. Try the sandbox with a made up shop. Runs in your browser, nothing gets saved.

    Open demo: Tool Ticket System

    Doc Extract

    Drop in an invoice, estimate, photo, or scan. It pulls out the vendor, dates, amounts, and job details so you can check them and export them. Try a sample doc or one of your own. Runs in your browser, nothing gets sent.

    Open demo: Doc Extract

  • Live

    File Organizer

    Point it at a messy pile of files. It suggests clear names, sorts them into folders by job and type, and sets aside copies and unknowns for a person to check. Try the sample pile or your own file names. Runs in your browser, nothing gets sent.

    Open demo: File Organizer

  • Live

    Steel Estimating

    Paste a member list or pick a sample job. It figures the weight of every piece from AISC shape weights, totals the tons, and prices it at your $/lb. Download the workbook, a printable summary, or JSON. Runs in your browser, nothing gets sent.
